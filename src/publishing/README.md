# Channel publications

Every verified same-story incoming report creates a new cumulative edition. Distinct related events remain separate posts and never contribute facts, sources or media to one another. Each edition must be sufficient on its own, with the full event summary, the union of source links and links to every earlier post. A repeated or source-only report does not silently edit the last post's sources. Old posts remain in the channel.

New editions use a canonical HTML text message with a ten-dot interest bar, Russian headline, details and source sections. New editions present the complete cumulative summary, followed by `Первоисточники`, `Вторичные источники` and `Предыдущие публикации`. The obsolete new/earlier headings are not rendered. Legacy history text remains in the body; maintenance removes only the old formatted service headings, not matching literal text in factual paragraphs. Previous-post links are oldest first. Non-empty review remarks appear as `Заметки проверяющего: <причина>`. Review is informational, not an admission gate. New sends still require the minimum score and current generation.

## Complete text and sources

Snapshots retain all text, labels, review notes and sources. There is no 90-source cap. `renderFullText` splits decoded text into messages within Telegram's 4096 UTF-16 and entity limits. It never cuts raw HTML or splits a surrogate pair. Each message uses at most 90 formatting/link entities, leaving room for a later update marker. The first message also reserves character space for that marker. Overflow becomes ordered replies to the canonical message, not truncated text or dropped links. Link previews are disabled and edits clear old inline keyboards.

Migration `200_publication_text_parts.sql` stores the rendered parts before the first request. Part zero is canonical. Delivery commits its Telegram ID before sending continuation replies. Every continuation has its own durable state, attempt, fingerprint, retry deadline and message ID. Confirmed parts are not sent again. Known rejections retry the frozen text. Interrupted or ambiguous requests become unknown and require review, never a blind resend. Manual-run success counts exclude incomplete text, just as they exclude incomplete media.

Source links come from current collected mentions, source-only reports and successfully read, relevant research documents. An edition also inherits its verified same-event ancestors' frozen sources. Related-story navigation is not inherited as evidence or as same-event ancestry. The writer's partial URL selection cannot hide eligible mention origins. Primary publications stay distinct from relays. Collected origin links retain their provenance even when a crawler cannot read them. Failed, expired, superseded or irrelevant fetched pages are not newly promoted to sources. Titles come from read content, not advertising anchors.

## All media, in reply batches

New publications send full text first, then every distinct selected photo and video as replies. There is no ten-item event limit and no preference that silently discards non-native formats. Compatible batches contain at most ten attachments each. JPEG/PNG photos and MP4 videos use native uploads; other original formats use document batches. Uploads retain the original bytes.

Before the canonical request, a durable media plan freezes all selected items and their temporary bytes. Each batch records its outcome and Telegram IDs. Successful uploads erase their temporary bytes immediately. Maintenance erases remaining bytes within seven days of the original download, including unknown sends. Hashes, outcomes and message IDs remain for review and authorized deletion. Media retries cannot complete an edition whose text continuations remain unsent.

Existing caption-first albums keep their frozen delivery kind and reconciliation rules. They are not resent or converted after authorization. A legacy caption uses the original 1024-unit renderer with at most three compact source links. New full publications never use that shortened caption presentation. Preparing a legacy album is allowed only when its entire media selection fits one compatible group; otherwise the plan uses text and complete reply batches instead of dropping items.

## Ancestor update markers

Only after the newest edition's canonical message, every continuation and every media batch are confirmed does the publisher stage `UPD: <latest actual post URL>` above each ancestor's title. All ancestors point directly to the newest complete edition, not merely to their immediate successor. An incomplete, failed or unknown delivery cannot become the update target.

Marker ancestry requires verified same-story classifications or verified edition edges. Legacy unclassified edges and frozen previous-publication links alone cannot authorize UPD. Explicit non-same classifications override old edges; quarantined same classifications cannot establish ancestry. Preparation selects only classification participants, historical navigation, existing markers and legacy heading cleanup candidates, not every published post. It locks and stages only changed candidates. Repeating maintenance with no changes creates no new snapshots or edits. A restart after final delivery can recover the missing marker work.

Marker edits preserve original text and sources. The canonical marker and bottom links share one desired snapshot. Navigation changes preserve all original body text, source labels and media. Full legacy captions are not shortened to make room. If a marker cannot fit without replacing text, it goes into a tracked overflow footer. Unknown edits must reconcile against the exact attempted snapshot and fingerprint before a newer marker can replace them. Known rejected edits retain Telegram's global cooldown when superseded.

## Related-story links and historical repair

Confirmed distinct related stories appear in `Связанные новости` at the bottom, after source and same-event previous-publication groups. New posts render these links inline and split only at Telegram limits. Once a related post is fully delivered, maintenance updates both directions, including already published posts. This does not regenerate headlines or summaries and does not imply an update to the event.

`prepareThreadUpdates` composes navigation and marker changes from the sent snapshot. It can remove an obsolete UPD when no verified same-event target remains. Legacy previous-post links become related only after explicit related classification. Otherwise they remain neutral `Другие публикации`, with their URLs and labels retained. Automatically generated related links disappear after explicit unrelated reclassification. Metadata-version stamping alone never triggers a Telegram edit.

Migration `204_publication_navigation_parts.sql` tracks edits to frozen continuation navigation tails and true limit-overflow replies. The planner locates navigation across part boundaries, including split formatted headings, without rewriting factual prefixes or original source links. It uses the final existing text part whenever the footer fits. Only caption, text or entity limits require a separate reply. Every operation shares the publication lock and attempt ledger, pins the authorizing snapshot, checks current admission, and preserves bot-wide 429 cooldowns. Unknown edits reconcile against their exact fingerprint. Unknown sends remain review-required and are never blindly repeated. Authorized deletion removes confirmed overflow replies before the canonical post and blocks on unknown navigation outcomes. Manual results expose incomplete navigation rather than counting it as finished.

## Retries, repairs and scheduling

Known rate-limit rejections resume after their saved cooldown. Proven DNS/connect failures retry because no HTTP request reached Telegram. Socket resets, response timeouts and other ambiguous failures remain unknown. Permanent failures do not retry. `TELEGRAM_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS` defaults to 30000, range 1000 through 300000. Multipart uploads use `TELEGRAM_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_MS`, default 300000, range 1000 through 900000. New attempts persist their deadline so reconciliation covers the actual request window without widening historical windows.

Canonical text sends reconcile against the original text/link fingerprint and attempt window. Legacy album reconciliation also requires the whole media group, expected count and first caption message. A matching caption alone cannot complete an album. Unknown edits reconcile before current freshness/admission checks and never authorize another request. Historical attempts keep their own snapshots even if a newer desired snapshot exists.

Automatic source-only edits are disabled. Explicit `sourceRepair` preparation preserves the sent headline, details, earlier summary, score and review. First-part repairs of new text publications must leave every continuation unchanged; otherwise preparation reports `publication_text_requires_new_edition` rather than silently truncating or rewriting the thread. A successful edit switches the snapshot pointer and saves the repaired canonical part. Unknown edits retain their attempted snapshot for reconciliation. Current settings, destination, bot ownership and cooldowns remain checked at dispatch.

`previewPublicationRepair` inspects tracked posts without writes. `repairExistingPublications` is an explicit repair path, not an incoming-report handler. Authorized duplicate or spurious-post removal deletes tracked continuations and media before the canonical message and records progress for retries. Unknown text/media outcomes block deletion. Unknown or untracked messages are never inferred deletion targets. Ordinary cumulative publication never removes ancestors. No Telegram callback polling runs.

`publishDigest` captures eligible ready events in one query. The default `digestCron` is `0 */3 * * *`. Events arriving during selection wait for the next digest. Manual-origin ownership prevents an automatic path around a manual run's cap. `startPublicationMaintenance` resumes prepared sends, text/media delivery, reconciliation, markers and authorized edits/deletions. It does not select new ready events. `PUBLICATION_READY_INTERVAL_MS` accepts 1000 to 60000 milliseconds, default 5000. See [timing and ownership](../../docs/immediate-publishing.md).
